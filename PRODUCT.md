# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Owners, general managers and finance managers of growing companies in Saudi Arabia and Egypt (contracting, trading, manufacturing, retail, real estate, restaurants, logistics, services). They run the business on spreadsheets, an outgrown local system, or a failed ERP attempt. They arrive from search, WhatsApp shares and sales follow-ups, often on a phone, often in Arabic. The job: find out whether Falcon can fix how their company runs, and talk to someone who knows their sector.

## Product Purpose
falcon-it.sa is the lead engine for Falcon's implementation and development team. It sells expertise, not a price list: Odoo implementation and custom development, and Falcon's own ERP (Desktop and Cloud). Every sector gets its own landing page showing that sector's problems, what we set up, and which system fits. No prices on the site. Success = qualified requests for a free fit session that ends in a written roadmap.

## Brand Personality
Expert, confident, direct. The voice of a senior implementer who has done this many times: few words, specific examples, no hype. Emotional goal: "these people understand my business, and they'll tell me the truth about which system I need." Reference feel: Odoo.com (clean, product-forward, confident headlines, real product screens), carried in Falcon's own blue identity.

## Anti-references
- The current falcon-it.sa (inconsistent numbers, placeholder testimonials, generic SaaS sections, prices).
- Ready-made SaaS templates: purple gradients, identical icon cards, eyebrow labels on every section.
- Typical Odoo-partner sites: stock photos of smiling staff, vague copy.
- Heavy government-style portals: dense, formal, boring.

## Design Principles
1. Show the product, not a promise: real Falcon and Odoo screens, real client logos, real sector workflows.
2. Sector first: every visitor should reach "their" page within one click.
3. Say it like an expert: short, specific, no unverifiable claims, no invented numbers.
4. One next step: the fit session is the single conversion; WhatsApp is the fast alternative.
5. Arabic is first-class: RTL layouts are designed, not mirrored afterthoughts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Full RTL/LTR parity. Readable on mid-range Android phones on mobile data (performance budget matters). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
